|===
|Code|Name|Definition

|author
|Author
|The creator of the content

|cameraOperator
|Camera Operator
|A person who operates the camera that records the content of the item

|contentSource
|Content Source	
|A party which contributed to the content of the item as a source

|correspondent
|Correspondent
|A person who is reporting information that contributes to the content of the item

|descrWriter
|Description Writer
|The person or party editing the description of content

|editor
|Editor
|The person or party editing the content and/or metadata of the item

|factChecker
|Fact Checker
|Specifies the person who fact-checked the news item to ensure its accuracy

|imgSupplier
|Image Supplier
|A party which supplied an image as content of the item

|interviewee
|Interviewee
|A person being interviewed for the item

|mediaOffice
|Media Office
|The media relationships office of a person, organisation or company

|origcont
|Content Originator
|A party which originated the content of the item

|originfo
|Information Originator
|A party which provided some information used to create or enhance the content of the item

|spokesperson
|Spokesperson
|The spokesperson of a person, organisation or company

|===
